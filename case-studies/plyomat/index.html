01 / Problem

Hardware measurements were only the starting point.

A coach needed more than numbers from a sensor. They needed to connect quickly, trust that a reading was live, repeat the same protocol across athletes, understand the result during training, and preserve it for comparison later.

01

Live measurement

Contact and flight events had to become contact time, jump height, RSI, threshold feedback, and timing controls without interrupting an active test.

02

Repeatable protocols

Standard and custom assessments needed their own repetition counts, ranges, thresholds, side-specific sequences, and best-result calculations.

03

Useful history

The workflow had to continue from measurement into athlete records, sortable reviews, quadrant interpretation, and unit-aware CSV sharing.

02 / Architecture

A pipeline from device to decision.

The app separates connection state, measurement parsing, assessment rules, presentation, and persistence so the same hardware stream can power live display, timer, assessments, review, and export.

03 / Hard BLE problem

Keep asynchronous data trustworthy.

Measurements, battery updates, connection changes, mode commands, and repetitions all move independently. Without explicit state, a stale characteristic or incomplete record could look like a valid live result.

A

Controlled connection lifecycle

The app filters for Plyomat hardware, handles Android bonding and MTU negotiation, discovers services, subscribes to the two required characteristics, and performs a controller handshake.

B

Commit complete repetitions

Incoming UTF-8 notifications are decoded once. Mode-specific parsers validate labeled contact, flight, height, and RSI fields before adding a repetition to reactive state.

C

Reset on disconnect

A disconnect clears active characteristics, handshake state, and transient repetitions so cached data is never presented as a new live measurement.

05 / Result

A field-ready workflow, shipped.

Plyomat 2.0 carries a coach from hardware connection through measurement, interpretation, saved history, and export in one mobile product, released on both iOS and Android.

01

Live performance metrics

Contact time, flight time, jump height, RSI, thresholds, rolling summaries, and contact-driven timing.

02

Repeatable assessment system

Standard and custom templates, bilateral tests, best-repetition logic, and four-quadrant visualization.

03

Durable coaching records

Local athlete history, sortable review, configurable units, and native CSV sharing.
