01 / The challenge

Security is complex.
The experience cannot be.

Physical access sits at the intersection of identity, proximity, hardware, and trust. The product had to coordinate every layer without asking the user to understand any of them.

A successful experience begins with the right organization, validates the user's account and phone, finds only the assigned nearby reader, and completes a cryptographic handshake—all from one deliberate tap.

01

Multi-tenant setup

One app needed to support organization-specific services and branding without a separate build.

02

Reader selection

Users should never choose from a noisy list of nearby BLE peripherals or technical identifiers.

03

Native protocol

Android and iOS expose different BLE APIs, but both had to produce the same authentication outcome.

06 / Reflection

What I would take
to production next.

01

Hardware-backed secret storage

Move credential-bearing material into Android Keystore and iOS Keychain with explicit lifecycle rules.

02

Full protocol verification

Use a cryptographically secure nonce for every attempt and validate the reader's proof, not only its finish marker.

03

Explicit offline policy

Define credential expiry, revocation, status refresh, and the precise conditions for offline authentication.

04

Known-answer test suite

Cover key derivation, AES messages, callback ordering, channel contracts, and fake-reader state transitions.

Deep protocol work,
